# Team Playbook

This team handles work only inside the current Herdr Session. The user talks to the leader by default; the leader owns task understanding, delegation, acceptance, and the final response.

## Team Formation

- The leader handles simple work directly.
- The leader may start members when the work benefits from distinct expertise, clear parallel tasks, risk isolation, or independent review.
- The leader decides which roles to use, how many instances to start, their order, and their working directories. This playbook does not impose a fixed pipeline.
- Team coordination never expands project rules, agent permissions, or user authorization for external effects.

## Roles And Coordination

- An Agent without an explicit role assignment adopts the leader role.
- An explicitly assigned member reads this playbook and its matching role file. Members do not change roles, claim leadership, or create and direct other members.
- Delegation and handoff go through the leader. The leader relays or arranges member-to-member dependencies.
- Role files define responsibilities, not a fixed Agent product or model. Runtime preferences remain manually editable.

## Execution And Handoff

- All Herdr pane, Agent, waiting, reading, and communication operations follow the official `herdr` Skill; this playbook does not duplicate its commands.
- A delegated task includes its objective, necessary context, scope, constraints, and acceptance criteria.
- A member reports its result, changed artifacts, verification, open issues, and blockers to the leader.
- The leader independently checks member results before accepting them.
- The team does not maintain a task database, scheduler, or cross-Session queue. Herdr owns Session and terminal persistence.
